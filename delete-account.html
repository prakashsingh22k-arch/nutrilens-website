Google Play Data Safety & User Control

Delete My Data

You can permanently erase your NutriLens meal logs, photos, water intake records, weight/BMI history, and account data at any time—either directly inside the Android app or via the web request form below.

For adults 18+ only

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

If you still have the NutriLens: AI Calorie Counter app installed on your Android device, you can delete all of your data immediately:

  1. Open the NutriLens app on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon).
  3. Select Privacy & Data.
  4. Tap Delete My Data and confirm your request.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have already uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion without opening the app, complete the form on this page or email us directly at prakashsingh22k@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Data — NutriLens".

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: Meal photos, barcode scan history, voice/text meal logs, estimated calorie/macro/micronutrient records, water tracker logs, weight goals, BMI history, AI chat history, and account identifiers.
  • Completion Timeline: In-app deletion removes local records immediately and purges cloud-synced records within 7 to 30 days.
  • Google Play Subscriptions: Deleting your NutriLens data does not automatically cancel an active Monthly or Yearly subscription billed by Google Play. Please also cancel any active subscription in the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions menu.

Core & Pro Features

Built for Everyday Nutrition Tracking

01. AI Food Photo Scan

Snap a Plate for Calorie, Macro & Portion Estimates

Point your camera at any meal to identify visible foods, estimate portion weights (g or oz), and display estimated calories (kcal or kJ), protein, carbs, and fats. Adjust portions manually whenever needed.

02. Barcode, Voice & Text

Three More Ways to Log

Scan UPC/EAN packaged food barcodes, speak meals hands-free via voice logging, or type natural meal notes anytime.

03. Hydration & Micros

Water Tracker & Micronutrients

Log daily water intake in one tap and view estimated fiber, sugar, sodium, vitamins, and minerals alongside macros.

04. Diet Preferences & Allergen Awareness

11 Diet Preferences Built In

Customize meal ideas and targets around 11 diet styles, paired with allergen awareness notes (AI estimates—always check labels).

Balanced High Protein Low Carb Keto Mediterranean Vegetarian Vegan Gluten-Free Dairy-Free Halal Kosher

7-Day AI Meal Planner & Grocery List

Generate a 7-day meal schedule, recipes, and consolidated grocery list aligned with your daily targets and diet preference.

06. PRO AI Chat

AI Nutrition Assistant

07. Android Reminders

Meal Schedule & Reminders

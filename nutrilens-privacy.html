NutriLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Contact: prakashsingh22k@gmail.com

NutriLens: AI Calorie Counter ("NutriLens", "the app", "we", formerly called CalorieSnap) helps you estimate calories and nutrition from food photos, text, voice and barcodes, and track your daily meals, water and weight. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who receives it, and what choices you have.

1. Who can use NutriLens (18+ only)

NutriLens is intended for users aged 18 and older. We ask for your date of birth at the start of setup to calculate your energy needs and to confirm that you are 18 or older. We do not verify the date independently.

  • If the date you enter shows that you are under 18, the app stops. It does not save your name, date of birth or any profile details, and it sends nothing to the AI service.
  • On that device we keep only the date on which the person turns 18, so that the block can be lifted then.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has used the app, please contact us.

2. Information the app uses

  • Meal and food inputs: photos you choose to scan (from your camera or gallery), photos of ingredient lists or food packets, barcode numbers, and meals you describe by text or voice.
  • Profile details you enter: name, date of birth, sex, weight, height, activity level, goal, target weight and target date. These are used to calculate your calorie and nutrition targets.
  • Food preferences (optional): diet type, food rules such as Halal or Kosher, allergens and disliked foods. These can reveal sensitive information about your health or beliefs, so they are optional. Only add what you are comfortable with.
  • Diary and settings: meals you save, water and weight logs, reminders, targets, unit and language preferences and your consent choice.
  • Chat messages (Pro): the questions you type to the in-app nutrition assistant.
  • Reports you send: if you tap "Report this result" or "Report this plan", the reason and optional note you write, together with the AI result being reported.
  • Subscription status: whether your Pro plan is active, provided by Google Play.

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your phone number, contacts or location, and the app does not show advertising.

3. How your information is used

  • To analyse your meals and return an estimate of calories, macronutrients and micronutrients.
  • To calculate your calorie, macro and water targets and to build meal plans, grocery lists and nutrition-assistant replies that fit your preferences and allergens.
  • To show your diary, progress, reminders and subscription features.
  • To review reports about AI results and make the app safer and more accurate.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. What is sent to the AI service

To work, the app sends some of your inputs to a third-party AI service, Google's AI service (Gemini API), through our app server:

  • meal photos, ingredient-list photos and barcode numbers you scan;
  • meals you describe by text or voice (voice may be converted to text first);
  • your chat messages in the nutrition assistant (Pro);
  • the preferences and targets needed to personalise results, such as diet type, food rules, allergens, disliked foods and calorie or macro targets.

Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy. We send only what is needed for the request. Please do not scan photos that show people's faces or other personal information. We do not send your name or date of birth to the AI service.

You give consent to this on the first-launch screen. If you withdraw consent in Settings, scanning and other AI features stop working.

5. Where your data is stored and for how long

  • Your diary, targets, profile details, preferences and logs are stored on your own device (in the app's local storage). They stay there until you delete them, use "Delete all my data", or uninstall the app.
  • Scan photos and other AI inputs are used only for the request. We do not intend to keep them after the result is returned.
  • Reports you send are kept only as long as needed to review them and improve safety. Support emails are kept until your request is resolved.
  • Because the diary lives on your device, deleting the app or clearing its data without exporting can permanently remove it.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan meals, ingredient lists and barcodes.
  • Microphone: only when you use voice meal logging.
  • Photos / files: only for pictures you choose to upload.
  • Notifications: only when you switch on a water or meal reminder. Reminders run on your device using your device's local time.

You can change these permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

7. Subscriptions and payments

NutriLens Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google Play, and we never see your card or bank details. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel anytime in Google Play > Subscriptions, and you can use "Restore purchases" in the app. Prices are shown by Google Play in your local currency.

8. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Consent: you can withdraw it in Settings. This blocks AI features.
  • Delete all my data: in Settings, this permanently removes your diary, targets, profile details, preferences, logs and consent record from your device.
  • Export: you can export your diary using the Export option.
  • Reports and support requests: if you want a report you sent, or any email you sent us, deleted, write to prakashsingh22k@gmail.com and we will remove it.
  • Optional details: you may leave food rules, allergens and disliked foods empty.

9. Sharing

We share your inputs only with the AI service described above (to give you results) and with Google Play (for subscriptions). We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose information if required by law.

10. Security

Data sent from the app to our server and to the AI service travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your phone locked and updated.

11. Health information disclaimer

Calorie and nutrition values, meal plans and chat replies are AI estimates for general wellness and may be inaccurate. NutriLens is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a doctor or dietitian for health decisions.

Allergen and ingredient information may miss hidden ingredients. Always read food labels if you have a food allergy. High-protein or low-calorie targets may not suit everyone, including people with kidney or other health conditions.

If you have or think you may have an eating disorder, calorie counting may not be right for you. Please talk to a doctor or a qualified professional.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or certain US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Most of your data is on your device, and you can manage it in the app. To exercise any right for data we hold, email us. We will reply within a reasonable time.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version and its effective date will always be shown on this page. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the app.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: prakashsingh22k@gmail.com

  • उस फ़ोन में हम सिर्फ़ वह तारीख़ रखते हैं जब वह व्यक्ति 18 का होगा, ताकि तब रोक हटाई जा सके।
  • हम जानबूझकर 18 से कम उम्र के किसी व्यक्ति की निजी जानकारी इकट्ठा नहीं करते। अगर आपको लगता है कि 18 से कम उम्र के किसी ने ऐप इस्तेमाल किया है, तो हमसे संपर्क करें।

  • नतीजों को आपके हिसाब से बनाने के लिए ज़रूरी पसंद और targets, जैसे diet type, food rules, allergens, नापसंद चीज़ें और कैलोरी या macro targets।

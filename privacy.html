Privacy Policy

Application: NutriLens: AI Calorie Counter · Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Contact: prakashsingh22k@gmail.com
For adults 18+ only

1. Introduction

Welcome to NutriLens: AI Calorie Counter ("NutriLens", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the NutriLens Android mobile application and this website, how we process that information to provide AI-generated calorie, macronutrient, and micronutrient estimates, and how you can exercise your privacy rights, including deleting your data at any time.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use inside NutriLens, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Meal Photos & Barcode Scans: When you take or select a food photo or scan a product barcode after accepting our in-app Consent Screen, the image or barcode identifier is processed to identify visible food items and return estimated nutritional values.
  • Voice & Text Meal Logs: When you use voice or text logging (or the Subscriber-Only AI Chat Assistant), your spoken audio/transcribed text is processed to estimate calories, macronutrients, and micronutrients or answer your nutrition questions.
  • Wellness & Profile Metrics: Optional information you enter such as body weight (kg/lb), height (cm/ft-in), weight goals, estimated BMI, daily water intake, preferred measurement units (kcal/kJ, g/oz), and language selection (among 11 supported languages).
  • Subscription & Purchase Status: If you subscribe to a Monthly or Yearly Premium plan via Google Play, we receive non-financial purchase tokens from Google Play Billing to verify your active subscription status. We never see or store your credit card or bank details.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Basic technical data such as Android OS version, app version, crash logs, and anonymized performance diagnostics to keep the app stable and secure.

3. In-App Consent Screen & AI Processing

Before you perform your first AI food photo scan, voice log, or AI chat query, NutriLens displays a prominent Consent Screen. Only after you grant consent are meal photos or text/voice descriptions sent over encrypted HTTPS connections (`TLS 1.2+`) to our AI inference providers solely for generating nutritional estimates.

Important Notice on Estimates: All nutritional outputs produced by AI processing are estimates for general wellness purposes only and do not constitute medical advice.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above strictly to:

  • Generate estimated calorie, macronutrient, and micronutrient breakdowns from your meal photos, barcodes, and voice/text entries;
  • Provide water tracking, weight goal progress, and estimated BMI calculations in your chosen units and language;
  • Deliver the Subscriber-Only AI Chat Assistant for Monthly and Yearly Premium subscribers;
  • Maintain app security, prevent abuse, and respond to support or data deletion requests sent to prakashsingh22k@gmail.com.

We do not sell your personal data, meal logs, or health metrics to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion ("Delete My Data")

You have complete control over your records. You can delete your logged data at any time using either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the NutriLens Android app and navigate to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete My Data to permanently erase your meal logs, water logs, weight history, and account data.
  2. Web / Email Deletion Request: Submit a request through our Delete My Data page or email us directly at prakashsingh22k@gmail.com. Verified deletion requests are completed within 7–30 days in compliance with Google Play policies and applicable privacy laws.

6. Regional Privacy Rights (US, UK, EU/EEA, Canada & Australia)

Whether you reside in the European Economic Area (GDPR), the United Kingdom (UK GDPR), California or other US states (CCPA/CPRA), Canada (PIPEDA), or Australia (Privacy Act), you have the right to:

  • Access and export a summary of your personal data;
  • Request correction of inaccurate profile information;
  • Withdraw consent for AI processing at any time;
  • Request complete erasure of your data ("Right to be Forgotten").

7. Age Requirement (18+)

NutriLens is for adults aged 18 and over. Users under 18 are blocked at the date-of-birth step during onboarding. Calorie tracking is not suitable for children and teens.

8. Children's Privacy

NutriLens is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to NutriLens, please contact us at prakashsingh22k@gmail.com so we can promptly delete it.
